🤖 AI Role-Play Scenarios

Practice each day's scenario with an AI assistant

Day 01 — Opening a Meeting with Vendor

Научиться уверенно открывать встречу с SAP-подрядчиком

Prompt for AI:

Role-play a meeting scenario: "Opening a Meeting with Vendor". You are the Vendor. I am the Customer. Use the following phrases in our conversation:

  • Good morning / afternoon everyone. Thanks for joining the call.
  • Thank you for the proposal you sent. We've reviewed it internally.
  • Let's start with the agenda. First topic is a new requirement from the regulator.
  • We have participants from both our compliance team and your SAP team today.
  • Could you briefly introduce your team members?

Day 02 — Assigning Tasks to Vendor

Ставить задачи SAP-подрядчику: требования, оценка, сроки

Prompt for AI:

Role-play a meeting scenario: "Assigning Tasks to Vendor". You are the Vendor. I am the Customer. Use the following phrases in our conversation:

  • We have a new business requirement from the regulator.
  • Could you assess the impact and give us an estimate?
  • We need a solution for Chestny Znak. Please prepare a proposal.
  • What is the timeline for the regulatory deadline?
  • Please provide a cost estimate for this change.

Day 03 — Change Request Due to Regulation

Сообщать подрядчику об изменениях госорганов и инициировать Change Request

Prompt for AI:

Role-play a meeting scenario: "Change Request Due to Regulation". You are the Vendor. I am the Customer. Use the following phrases in our conversation:

  • The regulator updated the requirements. We need to change the scope.
  • This is a new government requirement. We must implement by [date].
  • How will this affect the timeline and budget?
  • Please provide a revised proposal with the new scope.
  • We need a formal Change Request to update the contract.

Day 04 — Q&A and Clarifying with Vendor

Переспрашивать, уточнять и просить объяснить проще, когда подрядчик говорит сложно или быстро

Prompt for AI:

Role-play a meeting scenario: "Q&A and Clarifying with Vendor". You are the Vendor. I am the Customer. Use the following phrases in our conversation:

  • Sorry, could you repeat that more slowly?
  • Could you explain that in simpler terms?
  • I didn't quite catch that. Could you say it again?
  • What does 'API endpoint' mean in this context?
  • Could you give me an example?

Day 05 — Closing Meeting with Vendor

Закрывать встречу: резюмировать, назначать задачи, фиксировать дедлайны

Prompt for AI:

Role-play a meeting scenario: "Closing Meeting with Vendor". You are the Vendor. I am the Customer. Use the following phrases in our conversation:

  • Let me summarize what we've agreed on today.
  • Our main action item is the impact assessment. John, you own this.
  • Please send the minutes to everyone by tomorrow.
  • The deadline for the proposal is next Friday, July 3rd.
  • Let's schedule a follow-up for next Tuesday to review.

Day 06 — Small Talk with Vendor

Делать small talk с SAP-подрядчиком перед деловой частью

Day 07 — Handling Complaints to Vendor

Выражать недовольство подрядчику конструктивно: срыв сроков, качество

Prompt for AI:

Role-play a meeting scenario: "Handling Complaints to Vendor". You are the Vendor. I am the Customer. Use the following phrases in our conversation:

  • I need to raise a concern about the timeline.
  • The quality of the latest deliverable is not what we expected.
  • We agreed on July 3rd. My team is blocked without this.
  • We found several issues in the test results. Let me walk you through them.
  • What went wrong, and how will you fix it?

Day 08 — Scope Disputes with Vendor

Обсуждать спорные вопросы: scope, change order, компромиссы

Prompt for AI:

Role-play a meeting scenario: "Scope Disputes with Vendor". You are the Vendor. I am the Customer. Use the following phrases in our conversation:

  • We believe this is within the current project scope.
  • I disagree. This was not part of the original agreement.
  • We need to raise a Change Request for this new requirement.
  • Can we find a compromise? Perhaps share the cost 50/50?
  • Let's look at the contract. Section 3.2 covers regulatory changes.

Day 09 — Summarizing Vendor Discussions

Структурировать хаотичное обсуждение с подрядчиком

Prompt for AI:

Role-play a meeting scenario: "Summarizing Vendor Discussions". You are the Vendor. I am the Customer. Use the following phrases in our conversation:

  • Let me stop you there. I want to make sure I understand.
  • So if I understand correctly, there are three main issues.
  • Let's take one topic at a time.
  • I want to capture this. Can you repeat the key point?
  • Let's structure this. First point: the API change. Second: testing.

Day 11 — Full Meeting: New SAP Requirement

Полная встреча: открытие → постановка задачи → обсуждение → резюме → задачи

Prompt for AI:

Role-play a meeting scenario: "Full Meeting: New SAP Requirement". You are the Vendor. I am the Customer. Use the following phrases in our conversation:

  • Good morning everyone. Thanks for joining today's call.
  • We have a new requirement for the Mercury VSD integration.
  • The regulator now requires VSD numbers in the XML format before shipment.
  • What is the impact on our current process?
  • Which systems need changes — ECC, PI, or both?

Day 12 — Full Meeting: Critical SAP Incident

Экстренная встреча по критическому инциденту с подрядчиком

Prompt for AI:

Role-play a meeting scenario: "Full Meeting: Critical SAP Incident". You are the Vendor. I am the Customer. Use the following phrases in our conversation:
