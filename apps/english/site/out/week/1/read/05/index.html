Closing Vendor Meetings Effectively

Customer ↔ Vendor

🧑‍💼 Customer
Let me summarize what we've agreed on today.
🤝 Vendor
That makes sense. We've seen similar requests from other clients and have a standard approach for this.
🧑‍💼 Customer
Please send the minutes to everyone by tomorrow.
🤝 Vendor
I appreciate you raising this. Let me propose a solution that should address your concerns.
🧑‍💼 Customer
Let's schedule a follow-up for next Tuesday to review.
🤝 Vendor
I see your point. Let me suggest a compromise that could work for both sides.
🧑‍💼 Customer
If there are no questions, we can wrap up here.
