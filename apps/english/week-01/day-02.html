🎭 AI-практика

Scenario 1 — Push for clarity

You are a support specialist. I am a manager asking for an update on a Mercury integration issue. Give me a vague answer like "it's being looked at" and see if I ask you for specifics — timelines, blockers, next steps. If I do, give me real details. Start.

Scenario 2 — Track multiple items

You are a developer managing three tasks: SAP IDoc fix (almost done), MES batch job (blocked), and Честный ЗНАК emission (waiting for CRPT). I am a manager. Ask me for status on each. I will give you short answers — push me for more details. Start the meeting.

Reading: The Weekly Sync

The weekly status sync is the heartbeat of any project. Its goal is not to micromanage but to surface blockers early. Each team member reports briefly on: what they completed, what they are working on next, and any obstacles. A good status update is specific and concise. Instead of "working on SAP," say "completed IDoc mapping validation, moving to error handling logic." This keeps the team aligned and the manager informed without wasting time.

Еженедельный статус-митинг — это сердце любого проекта. Его цель не микроменеджмент, а раннее выявление блокеров. Каждый участник кратко сообщает: что сделано, что в работе и какие есть препятствия. Хороший статус-апдейт конкретен и краток.
