Thank you for the proposal you sent. We've reviewed it internally.Спасибо за предложение. Мы его внутренне рассмотрели.
Let's start with the agenda. First topic is a new requirement from the regulator.Давайте начнём с повестки. Первая тема — новое требование от регулятора.
We have participants from both our compliance team and your SAP team today.Сегодня у нас участники от нашего комплаенса и вашей SAP-команды.
Could you briefly introduce your team members?Можете кратко представить участников вашей команды?
The main goal of this meeting is to discuss the new regulatory change.Основная цель встречи — обсудить новое изменение регулятора.
I'll keep the meeting to 45 minutes to respect everyone's time.Я уложу встречу в 45 минут.
Has everyone received the updated requirements document I sent earlier?Все получили обновлённый документ с требованиями?
Feel free to stop me if you have questions at any point.Не стесняйтесь останавливать меня при вопросах.
Let me share my screen to walk through the requirements.Позвольте показать экран и пройтись по требованиям.

🗣️ Диалог для отработки

Прочитай вслух по ролям (ты — Customer). Потом перескажи от своего лица.

You (Customer): Good morning everyone, thanks for joining. Let's start with the agenda. First topic is the new Chestny Znak requirement. Second, the impact on the SAP interface. John, has your team received the document I sent on Friday?

John: Yes, we received it. We have some clarifying questions.

Customer: Good. Let's go through the requirements first. Sarah from our compliance team will explain the regulatory background.

Reading: Kickoff Meetings with SAP Vendors

A kickoff meeting with a new SAP vendor sets the foundation for the entire project. As a customer, your role is to clearly communicate the business requirements, regulatory deadlines, and expectations. Start by introducing all participants from both sides. State the agenda upfront. Share relevant documents. Give the vendor a chance to ask clarifying questions early. A good kickoff prevents misunderstandings later. The most important rule: do not assume the vendor knows your internal processes. Explain your compliance requirements, decision-making process, and key stakeholders. Agree on communication cadence. End with clear next steps and owners.

Кик-офф с новым SAP-подрядчиком закладывает основу проекта. Ваша роль как заказчика — чётко изложить требования и ожидания.
