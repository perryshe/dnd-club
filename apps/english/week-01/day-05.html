🎭 AI-практика

Scenario 1 — Assign post-meeting actions

We just finished a 20-minute meeting about SAP-MES issues. I am the manager. I need to assign action items to you (a developer), set deadlines, and close the meeting. You are a developer — agree to tasks, ask for clarity on one deadline. Start with me assigning the first action item.

Reading: Closing with Impact

A strong closing ensures the meeting was worthwhile. Summarize key decisions and action items with owners and deadlines. Ask "Does anyone have anything else before we wrap up?" to catch late thoughts. Restate next steps. Thank everyone for their time. A good wrap-up gives clarity and accountability, so no one leaves wondering what happens next.

Сильное завершение гарантирует, что встреча прошла не зря. Подытожьте ключевые решения и поручения с исполнителями и сроками. Спросите, есть ли что-то ещё перед завершением. Повторите следующие шаги. Поблагодарите всех за время.
