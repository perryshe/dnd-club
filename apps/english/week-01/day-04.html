🎭 AI-практика

Scenario 1 — Confusing explanation

You are a developer explaining why an interface failed. Use technical jargon and vague terms. I am a manager who needs to understand the real issue. Ask me clarifying questions to get to the root cause. Start with your confusing explanation.

Scenario 2 — Misunderstanding

You are a colleague who misunderstood a previous email. You think we agreed to change the server, but actually we agreed to change the user roles. I am the manager. We need to clarify this during the call. Start the conversation with your misunderstanding.

🗣️ Диалог для отработки

Developer: So yeah, the issue is that the IDoc was stuck because the logical system name changed after the transport. We need to adjust the partner profile.

Manager (You): Sorry, could you slow down a bit? Just to clarify — you're talking about the logical system name, not the RFC destination, correct?

Developer: Both actually. The logical system name affects the partner profile, which is what we use in the RFC configuration.

Manager: I want to make sure I understand. The transport changed the logical system name, which broke the partner profile, which caused the IDocs to fail. Is that the sequence?

Developer: Exactly. We need to update the partner profile in the production system.

Manager: Understood. And the impact — which IDocs are affected? Only the ones after the transport?

Developer: Yes, about 15 IDocs from last night failed. The ones before that are fine.

Manager: Let me check if I've got this right — you need to update the partner profile in production and re-process the 15 failed IDocs. What's the ETA?

Developer: Two hours, including testing.

Manager: Perfect. Thank you for clarifying.

Reading: The Art of Q&A

Questions are a manager's most powerful tool. Open-ended questions ("What do you think?") encourage discussion. Closed questions ("Is this ready?") confirm facts. Probing questions dig deeper. Clarifying questions prevent misunderstandings. During Q&A, listen actively, acknowledge each question, and if you don't know the answer, say "I'll follow up on that after the meeting." Strong Q&A builds trust.

Вопросы — самый мощный инструмент менеджера. Открытые вопросы поощряют дискуссию. Закрытые подтверждают факты. Углубляющие вопросы копают глубже. Уточняющие предотвращают недопонимание. Хороший Q&A укрепляет доверие.
