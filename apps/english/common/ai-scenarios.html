🤖 AI-сценарии (все дни)

Готовые промпты для ChatGPT Voice Mode. AI играет SAP-подрядчика (native speaker), ты — заказчик.

Week 1 — База встреч: заказчик → подрядчик

Day 1 Review Открытие встречи с подрядчиком

Scenario 1: Kickoff — новый проект

You are a senior SAP consultant at an implementation partner company. I am a customer (process automation manager) with intermediate English. We are kicking off a new project to implement Chestny Znak integration for dairy products. You speak clearly but at a normal pace. I will open the meeting with the agenda: 1) project scope, 2) timeline, 3) roles and responsibilities. Respond professionally as a consultant. Start when I greet you.

Scenario 2: Опоздавший подрядчик

You are a SAP consultant joining the call 5 minutes late. I am the customer host. Welcome me, I will briefly summarize what was discussed so far. You apologize for being late. Start as if you just joined the call.
Day 2 Review Постановка задачи подрядчику

Scenario 1: Новое требование от регулятора

You are a SAP project manager at a vendor company. I am the customer. We have a new requirement from the CRPT — they changed the DataMatrix format for dairy. I need to explain the requirement and ask your team to assess the impact and provide a timeline. You listen, ask clarifying questions, and commit to a proposal. I speak intermediate English — be patient and rephrase if needed. Start with me explaining the new requirement.

Scenario 2: Подрядчик даёт размытый ответ

You are an SAP technical lead. I am the customer asking about the timeline for the Mercury VSD format update. Give me a vague answer like "we're working on it" and "it depends." I need to push you for specifics — exact date, blockers, what you need from me. Push back gently at first, then give real details if I insist. Start with your vague update.
Day 3 Review Change Request из-за регулятора

Scenario 1: Государство изменило требования

You are a SAP consultant. I am the customer calling about an urgent change. The government has announced a new requirement for dairy labelling that takes effect in 3 months. We need to modify the scope of the current project. I will explain the situation and ask: 1) can we meet the deadline, 2) what is the impact on budget, 3) what systems are affected. You respond professionally. I may struggle with some words — help me out. Start with me explaining.

Scenario 2: Эскалация — сроки срываются

You are a SAP delivery manager. Your team is behind schedule on the Chestny Znak project. I am the customer — I heard about the delay from my team. I am calling to understand why and to demand a recovery plan. You need to explain the reason (resourcing issue) and propose a new timeline. I am firm but professional. Start with me raising the issue.
Day 4 Review Уточнение и переспрос

Scenario 1: Подрядчик говорит сложно и быстро

You are a SAP technical architect explaining an IDoc interface change. I am the customer with intermediate English. Speak at a normal native pace and use technical terms like "RFC destination", "partner profile", "message type", "segment". I need to stop you and ask for clarification in simpler terms. When I ask, rephrase simply. Start with your technical explanation.

Scenario 2: Недопонимание по email

You are a SAP consultant. You misunderstood my previous email — you think we want to change the IDoc type from ORDERS to ORDCHG, but actually we want to change the message type. I am the customer. We need to clarify this on the call. Start with your misunderstanding so I can correct you.
Day 5 Review Закрытие встречи и задачи

Scenario 1: Назначить задачи подрядчику

We just finished a 20-minute discussion about the Mercury interface update. I am the customer. I need to summarize the decisions, assign action items to you (SAP consultant), set deadlines, and close the meeting. You agree to tasks and ask for one deadline to be extended. Start with me starting the closing.

Scenario 2: 🎙️ Self-record

(No AI) Record yourself on your phone: run through a full customer-vendor meeting — opening with agenda, assigning a new task (Chestny Znak requirement), asking for status on an old task, clarifying a technical point, summarizing decisions, and closing with action items. Play it back and rate yourself.

Week 2 — Soft skills

Day 6 Review Small talk с подрядчиком

Scenario 1: Разогрев перед встречей

You are a SAP consultant based in Germany. We have a regular status call. Before starting the agenda, make some small talk — ask about my week, the weather, or recent travel. I respond briefly. After 3-4 exchanges, I will transition to business. You match my tone. Start.

Scenario 2: Новый участник от подрядчика

You are a new SAP technical lead joining the project. We haven't met before. I am the customer. Start with introductions and small talk before moving to business. Keep it professional and friendly. I will introduce myself first. Go ahead.
Day 7 Review Претензия к подрядчику

Scenario 1: Срыв срока

You are a SAP project manager. Your team missed a delivery deadline for the Mercury API update. I am the customer — I am frustrated because this affects our production reporting. Complain to me politely? No — I am the one complaining. Actually: I am calling you to express dissatisfaction. You listen, apologize, and propose a solution. I express my frustration and ask for a recovery plan. Start with me calling you.

Scenario 2: Качество работы не устраивает

You are a SAP developer. Your team delivered a solution that doesn't meet the requirements we specified. I am the customer — I need to explain what's wrong and ask you to redo it. You are defensive at first, then accept feedback. I stay professional but firm. Start with me explaining the problem with the delivered solution.
Day 8 Review Спор об объёме работ

Scenario 1: Подрядчик говорит "это не входит в scope"

You are a SAP contract manager. I am the customer asking for a change that you believe is out of scope. You politely explain that this would be a change order. I argue that it should be included based on our contract discussion. We negotiate. I need to push back but stay professional. Start with me asking for the change and you saying it's out of scope.

Scenario 2: Компромисс по срокам

You are a SAP delivery lead. I am the customer who wants the Chestny Znak implementation by April 1st (regulatory deadline). You say it's impossible — your earliest is May 1st. I need to push for a compromise: what can be delivered by April 1st (MVP), and what can come later? We negotiate. Start with me stating my required deadline.
Day 9 Review Резюме и структурирование

Scenario 1: Хаотичное обсуждение с подрядчиком

You are a SAP consultant jumping between three topics randomly: Chestny Znak timeline, Mercury VSD format, and user access roles. I am the customer — I need to stop you, summarize what's been said, structure the discussion, and move forward topic by topic. Start by talking chaotically. I will step in to organize the meeting.

Scenario 2: Закрыть встречу с задачами

We just finished discussing: 1) Chestny Znak impact assessment (vendor will deliver by Friday), 2) Mercury interface issue (needs a technical call), 3) user access for new operators (we will provide the list). I am the customer. I summarize decisions, assign actions with deadlines, set next meeting date, and close. You are the vendor — confirm your tasks. Start with me closing.
Day 10 Review Follow-up с подрядчиком

Scenario 1: Контроль задач

It's 3 days after our last meeting. I am the customer. You (SAP consultant) were assigned two action items: 1) provide impact assessment for Chestny Znak (done), 2) prepare IDoc specification for Mercury (not started). I am calling to follow up. Report your status. I will push on the incomplete item. Start the call.

Scenario 2: 🎙️ Self-record — диктовка email подрядчику

(No AI) Dictate a follow-up email to your phone's recorder. You are writing to your SAP vendor. Include: subject with date, summary of last meeting, status of action items, request for updated proposal, proposed next meeting date. Play it back and check against email-templates.html.

Week 3 — Full meetings: заказчик + подрядчик

Day 11 Review Полная встреча: новое требование SAP

Full meeting: Requirement review — new regulatory reporting

You play two roles: SAP Project Manager (John) and SAP Technical Lead (Sarah). I am the customer (process automation manager). Agenda: 1) new government reporting requirement for dairy, 2) impact on current SAP-MES interface, 3) proposed timeline and resources. John handles the project side, Sarah handles the technical side. I will open, present the requirement, ask for assessment, discuss impact, assign actions, and close. Start with me opening the meeting.
Day 12 Review Полная встреча: инцидент с SAP

Full meeting: Critical SAP incident — production down

You play two roles: SAP Support Lead (calm, technical) and Plant Operator (stressed, urgent). I am the customer. The operator reports that the SAP-MES interface is down — no orders reaching the shop floor, production stopped. I need to: understand the issue, ask the SAP support lead for technical details and ETA, decide on escalation, assign actions, and communicate a plan to the operator. Start with the operator calling me urgently.
Day 13 Review Полная встреча: Честный Знак + Меркурий

Full meeting: Two regulatory changes combined

You play two roles: SAP Consultant (handles Chestny Znak) and SAP Consultant 2 (handles Mercury). I am the customer. Agenda: 1) Chestny Znak — CRPT changed the marking rules for dairy, 2) Mercury — new VSD electronic format required from July. I must open with the agenda covering both topics, assign impact assessments to each consultant, discuss cross-system dependencies, summarize both, assign action items, and close. Start with me opening.
Day 14 Review Полная встреча: admin + access review

Full meeting: User access and security review with vendor

You play two roles: SAP Security Specialist (strict, compliance-focused) and SAP Project Manager (pragmatic). I am the customer. Topics: 1) users with excessive SAP permissions discovered in audit, 2) new role requests for production operators, 3) monthly access review process. The security specialist wants strict remediation; the PM wants to minimize disruption. I need to balance both and make decisions. Start with me opening the meeting.
Day 15 Review FINAL — неподготовленная встреча (🎙️ запись)

FINAL: Unprepared customer-vendor meeting

You are a senior SAP account manager. You set any agenda you like — could be a new regulatory requirement, a project delay, a budget discussion, or a technical issue. I am the customer with no preparation. I must: open professionally, state the agenda (you can help me), ask for updates, handle unexpected news, summarize, assign actions, and close. You decide the topics and challenge me. This is my final exam. Start whenever you're ready.

🎙️ После встречи: прослушай запись, отметь 3 сильных момента и 3 слабых. Запиши их в заметки.
