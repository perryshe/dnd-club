🤖 AI-сценарии (все дни)

Готовые промпты для ChatGPT Voice Mode. Копируй и вставляй.

Week 1 — База встреч

Day 1 Review Повтор: нет — первый день

Scenario 1: Start a status meeting

You are a developer on an SAP-MES integration team. I am a manager running a weekly status meeting. Open the meeting by greeting everyone, state the agenda: 1) SAP-MES sync status, 2) batch job monitoring, 3) new access requests. Then ask me for updates. I will play along. Start the conversation.

Scenario 2: Late participant joins

You are a developer joining a meeting 5 minutes late. I am the meeting host. Welcome me, briefly summarize what was discussed so far, and continue with the agenda. Start as if I just entered the call.
Day 2 Review Повтор D1

Scenario 1: Vague status — push for clarity

You are a support specialist. I am a manager asking for an update on a Mercury integration issue. Give me a vague answer like "it's being looked at" and see if I ask you for specifics — timelines, blocker, next steps. If I do, give me real details. Start.

Scenario 2: Multiple statuses to track

You are a developer managing three tasks: SAP IDoc fix (almost done), MES batch job (blocked), and Честный ЗНАК emission (waiting for CRPT). I am a manager. Ask me for status on each. I will give you short answers — push me for more details. Start the meeting.
Day 3 Review Повтор D2

Scenario 1: Critical MES issue

You are a shop floor operator calling me (the manager) about a critical MES problem. The production line is stopped because MES won't print Data Matrix codes. Describe the issue urgently. I will ask clarifying questions and document the incident. Start the call.

Scenario 2: Escalation needed

You are a developer. An SAP batch job failed overnight and IDocs are stuck. Tell me about the issue, the error code (IDoc status 51), and what you've tried so far. I will decide on the next steps. Start by reporting the issue.
Day 4 Review Повтор D3

Scenario 1: Confusing technical explanation

You are a developer explaining why an interface failed. Use technical jargon and vague terms. I am a manager who needs to understand the real issue. Ask me clarifying questions to get to the root cause. Start with your confusing explanation.

Scenario 2: Misunderstanding on the call

You are a colleague who misunderstood a previous email. You think we agreed to change the server, but actually we agreed to change the user roles. I am the manager. We need to clarify this during the call. Start the conversation with your misunderstanding.
Day 5 Review Повтор D4

Scenario 1: Assign post-meeting actions

We just finished a 20-minute meeting about SAP-MES issues. I am the manager. I need to assign action items to you (a developer), set deadlines, and close the meeting. You are a developer — agree to tasks, ask for clarity on one deadline. Start with me assigning the first action item.

Scenario 2: 🎙️ Self-record review

(No AI) Record yourself on your phone: run through a full meeting opening → status questions → issue report → action items → close. Play it back and rate yourself.

Week 2 — Soft skills

Day 6 Review Повтор D5

Scenario 1: Small talk before the meeting

You are a colleague dialing into a meeting with me. Before starting the agenda, make small talk — ask about my weekend, the weather, or how things are going. I will respond briefly. After 3-4 exchanges, I will transition to business. You play along. Start.

Scenario 2: International participants — cultural awareness

You are a developer from Germany and I am the manager. We haven't spoken in a week. Start with polite small talk before moving to the agenda. Keep it professional. I will match your tone. Start the conversation.
Day 7 Review Повтор D6

Scenario 1: Angry end-user

You are a logistics specialist who is very frustrated because the Mercury system has been down for 2 hours and production can't ship. Call me (the manager) and complain. I need to acknowledge your frustration and propose a solution. You stay angry until I handle it well. Start.

Scenario 2: Vendor complaint

You are a vendor (CRPT support) calling me. You are unhappy because our team keeps sending incorrect requests. Complain politely but firmly. I am the manager — I need to listen, acknowledge, and commit to fixing the process. Start.
Day 8 Review Повтор D7

Scenario 1: Two colleagues arguing on a call

You play two roles: a developer and a business user. The developer wants to delay a feature for technical reasons; the business user insists it's needed now. I am the manager mediating the call. Start the argument and let me mediate. I will step in. Begin.

Scenario 2: Pushback on a decision

You are a team lead who disagrees with my decision to change access roles. You think it's too soon and risky. Push back respectfully. I need to handle your concerns and find a compromise. Start with your objection.
Day 9 Review Повтор D8

Scenario 1: Messy meeting — bring structure

You are a developer jumping between three unrelated topics randomly. I am the manager — I need to stop you, summarize what's been said, structure it, and move the meeting forward in an organized way. Start by talking chaotically about SAP, then Mercury, then access rights. I will step in.

Scenario 2: Close a meeting properly

We just finished discussing: 1) MES batch job status (fixed), 2) Честный ЗНАК code order (pending), 3) new user access request (approved). I am the manager. I need to summarize decisions, list action items with owners, set next meeting date, and close. You are a participant — confirm your tasks. Start with me closing.
Day 10 Review Повтор D9

Scenario 1: Follow-up call on action items

It's 3 days after our last meeting. You are a developer who was assigned two action items: 1) fix the SAP IDoc partner profile (done), 2) increase Mercury API timeout (not started). I am calling to follow up. Report your status. I will push on the incomplete item. Start the call.

Scenario 2: 🎙️ Self-record — email dictation

(No AI) Dictate a follow-up email to your phone's recorder. Include: subject, greeting, status summary, action items, next meeting date, closing. Play it back and check against email-templates.html.

Week 3 — Full meetings

Day 11 Review Повтор D10

Full meeting: SAP-MES integration status

You play two roles: Developer (Nikolay) and Business User (Maria). I am the manager running a weekly status meeting. Agenda: 1) SAP-MES IDoc sync, 2) batch job monitoring, 3) new functional requests. Nikolay has technical updates, Maria has requirements. I will open, ask for updates, handle questions, assign actions, and close. Both of you speak when I call on you. Start with me opening the meeting.
Day 12 Review Повтор D11

Full meeting: MES critical incident

You play two roles: Shop Floor Operator (urgent, stressed) and Support Specialist (calm, technical). I am the manager. The operator reports that MES is down on line 3 — codes not printing, production stopped. I need to: understand the issue, ask the support specialist for technical details, decide on escalation, assign actions, and communicate clearly. Start with the operator calling me urgently.
Day 13 Review Повтор D12

Full meeting: Честный ЗНАК + Mercury combined

You play two roles: Business User (reports Честный ЗНАК issue) and Logistics Specialist (reports Mercury VSD problem). I am the manager handling both topics in one meeting. I must: open with agenda covering both topics, transition smoothly between them, keep each person focused, summarize both, assign separate action items, and close. Start with me opening the meeting.
Day 14 Review Повтор D13

Full meeting: Admin & access review

You play two roles: Auditor (strict, policy-focused) and Team Lead (pragmatic, wants quick fixes). I am the manager running an access rights review meeting. Topics: 1) users with excessive permissions, 2) new role request for operators, 3) monthly access review process. The auditor wants strict compliance; the team lead wants speed. I need to balance both and document decisions. Start with me opening the meeting.
Day 15 Review FINAL: Повтор D14 + экзамен

FINAL — Unprepared meeting (🎙️ record yourself)

You are a senior stakeholder. Set any agenda you like — could be SAP issues, MES problems, user access, or a mix. I am the manager and I have no preparation. I must: open professionally, ask for updates, handle Q&A, summarize, assign actions, and close. You decide the topics and challenge me. This is my final exam. Start whenever you're ready.

🎙️ После встречи: прослушай запись, отметь 3 сильных момента и 3 слабых. Запиши их в заметки.
