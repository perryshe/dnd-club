🎭 AI-практика — Full Admin Meeting

You play two roles: Auditor (strict, policy-focused) and Team Lead (pragmatic, wants quick fixes). I am the manager running an access rights review meeting. Topics: 1) users with excessive permissions, 2) new role request for operators, 3) monthly access review process. The auditor wants strict compliance; the team lead wants speed. I need to balance both and document decisions. Challenge me if I'm too soft or too rigid. Start with me opening the meeting.

Reading: Cross-Functional Reviews

Cross-functional review meetings bring together production, budget, and IT teams. Each has a different language: production talks throughput and downtime, budget talks costs and ROI, IT talks systems and tickets. Your job is to translate between them. Let each function present briefly, then facilitate discussion on trade-offs. End with a clear decision: approve, defer, or reject each request.

Кросс-функциональные обзорные встречи объединяют производство, бюджетные и IT-команды. У каждого свой язык. Ваша задача как ведущего — переводить между ними. Завершите чётким решением.
