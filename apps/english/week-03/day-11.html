📘 Освежение — Meeting Flow Checklist

Держи в голове последовательность встречи:

  1. Open: Greet → Agenda → Set expectations
  2. Status: Ask for updates → Dig into blockers → Check timelines
  3. Issues: Clarify → Document → Decide on next steps
  4. Close: Summarize → Assign actions → Confirm deadlines → Next meeting → Thanks

🎭 AI-практика — Full Meeting (30–40 мин)

Single long scenario

You play two roles: Developer (Nikolay) and Business User (Maria). I am the manager running a weekly status meeting. Agenda: 1) SAP-MES IDoc sync — Nikolay reports 3 IDocs failed last night, 2) batch job monitoring — Maria says one job is running slow, 3) new functional requests — Maria needs a new report. I will open, ask for updates, handle issues, assign actions, and close. Both of you speak when I call on you. If I handle each point well, move forward. If I'm unclear, ask questions. Start with me opening the meeting.

Reading: SAP-MES Integration

SAP and MES integration is the backbone of modern manufacturing. SAP ERP handles planning and orders, while MES manages real-time production on the shop floor. The two systems communicate through IDocs. When an IDoc fails, orders can be delayed or duplicated. A status meeting for this integration should cover: IDoc error rates, batch job health, and any pending change requests.

Интеграция SAP и MES — это основа современного производства. SAP ERP отвечает за планирование и заказы, а MES управляет производством в реальном времени. Две системы обмениваются через IDoc.
