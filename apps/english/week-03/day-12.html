🎭 AI-практика — Full Incident Meeting

You play two roles: Shop Floor Operator (urgent, stressed) and Support Specialist (calm, technical). I am the manager. The operator reports that MES is down on line 3 — codes not printing, production stopped. I need to: understand the issue, ask the support specialist for technical details, decide on escalation, assign actions, and communicate clearly. Challenge me if I'm unclear or slow. Start with the operator calling me urgently.

Reading: MES Incidents

MES incidents disrupt production and require swift action. Common issues include: printer failures on the line, label template errors, batch job timeouts, and data sync delays. Your role as a manager is to triage: Is this a production stop? Can we work around it? Who has the expertise to fix it? After resolution, a root cause analysis should be written to prevent recurrence.

Инциденты MES нарушают производство и требуют быстрых действий. Частые проблемы: отказ принтера на линии, ошибки шаблонов этикеток, таймауты пакетных заданий. Ваша роль как менеджера — провести триаж.
