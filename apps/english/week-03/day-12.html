Reading: Handling Critical SAP Incidents with Your Vendor

Critical incidents happen in SAP projects. The key is response speed. When an incident occurs, call the vendor immediately. Start with: 'We have a critical incident.' Describe the impact on business operations. Ask three questions: root cause, estimated fix time, workaround. Demand hourly updates. Require escalation if needed. After resolution, request a formal root cause analysis report. Keep a calm but firm tone. The vendor should feel the urgency without panic. Document the entire incident timeline for your records and to prevent recurrence.

При критическом инциденте звоните подрядчику немедленно. Спрашивайте причину, срок и обходное решение. Требуйте часовой отчёт и RCA.
