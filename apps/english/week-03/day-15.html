🗣️ Диалог для отработки

Прочитай вслух по ролям (ты — Customer). Потом перескажи от своего лица.

You (Customer): Hi John, thanks for taking this call on short notice. I have a topic I need to discuss urgently. We received a new clarification from the regulator about the Mercury integration timeline. The deadline moved from September 1st to August 15th.

Vendor: That's a significant change. We'll need to reprioritize resources.

Customer: Let's think together. What options do we have to meet the new deadline? Can we do a phased approach? I don't have all the details yet, but here's what I know. Can you prepare a quick assessment by end of day?

Vendor: Yes, I'll review the resource plan and get back to you by 5 PM.

Customer: Thanks, John. Let me note down the key points. I'll send a follow-up email with the details.

Reading: The Final Challenge: Unprepared Vendor Meetings

Day 15 is your final challenge. In real life, vendor meetings often happen without preparation — the regulator calls with an urgent change, a critical issue arises, or management asks for a last-minute status. The key is to stay calm and structured. Start with thanking the vendor for the short-notice call. State what you know and what you don't know. Ask the vendor to think together. Take notes during the call. End with a commitment to send a follow-up email. You have practiced all the skills from previous 14 days. Trust yourself. You are ready.

Финальный вызов: встреча без подготовки. Сохраняйте спокойствие, структурируйте обсуждение, записывайте ключевые моменты и отправляйте follow-up. Вы готовы!
