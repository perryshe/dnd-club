Scenario 1 — Two colleagues arguing

You play two roles: a developer and a business user. The developer wants to delay a feature for technical reasons; the business user insists it's needed now. I am the manager mediating the call. Start the argument and let me mediate. I will step in. Begin.

Scenario 2 — Pushback on a decision

You are a team lead who disagrees with my decision to change access roles. You think it's too soon and risky. Push back respectfully. I need to handle your concerns and find a compromise. Start with your objection.

🗣️ Диалог для отработки

Developer: I'm telling you, we can't push this feature now. The MES interface isn't stable enough. If we add more load, it will break.

Business User: We can't wait. The regulation deadline is next month. If we don't have the feature, we'll get fined.

Manager (You): I appreciate both perspectives. Let's pause and focus on the main goal here — we all want to meet the deadline without breaking the system. Let me reframe the question. Nikolay, what's the minimum we need to stabilize the interface?

Developer: At least two weeks of testing and a load balancing fix.

Manager: And Maria, what's the minimum feature set we need to avoid the fine?

Business User: Just the basic reporting. The advanced features can wait.

Manager: Good. So here's a proposal: we implement the reporting only by the deadline, and push the advanced features to the next release. Nikolay gets time to stabilize the interface, Maria gets the compliance feature. Does that work for both?

Developer: I can work with that.

Business User: Agreed.

Manager: Great. Action items: Nikolay, document the stabilization plan by Wednesday. Maria, confirm the reporting requirements by tomorrow. Let's reconvene Thursday for a quick check-in.

Reading: Constructive Conflict

Conflict in meetings is natural when smart people disagree. The manager's job is not to eliminate it but to channel it constructively. First, acknowledge both sides. Second, reframe the goal: "We all want the best outcome for production." Third, separate people from the problem. Ask "What data do we have?" not "Who is right?" If needed, park the issue. A skilled facilitator turns conflict into better decisions.

Конфликт на встречах естественен, когда умные люди расходятся во мнениях. Задача менеджера — не устранить его, а направить в конструктивное русло. Сначала признайте обе стороны. Затем переформулируйте цель. Отделяйте людей от проблемы.
