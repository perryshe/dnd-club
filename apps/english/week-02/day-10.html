🗣️ Диалог для отработки

Прочитай вслух по ролям (ты — Customer). Потом перескажи от своего лица.

You (Customer): Hi John, let's do a quick follow-up on our action items from last week. First, what's the status of the impact assessment? Is it on track for the July 3rd deadline?

Vendor: We're 80% done. Should be ready by Friday as planned.

Customer: Great. Second, I noticed we didn't receive the API documentation. What happened?

Vendor: Our technical writer was on sick leave. I can send a draft by Monday.

Customer: Okay, let's set a new deadline: Monday, July 6th. Do you need any support from our side to unblock this?

Reading: Effective Follow-ups with External Vendors

A follow-up meeting checks progress on agreed action items. Structure it simply: list 3-5 tasks from the last meeting, ask for status on each, and update deadlines if needed. Start with 'Let's do a quick follow-up on our action items from last week.' Ask specific questions: 'What's the status of X?' 'Is it on track for the deadline?' If a task is delayed, ask why, offer support, and agree on a new deadline. End by confirming the updated list. Regular follow-ups build accountability and help catch problems early before they become crises.

Регулярные follow-up помогают контролировать задачи и выявлять проблемы на ранней стадии. Спрашивайте статус, корректируйте сроки, предлагайте помощь.
