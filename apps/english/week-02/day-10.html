🎭 AI-практика

Scenario 1 — Follow-up call on action items

It's 3 days after our last meeting. You are a developer who was assigned two action items: 1) fix the SAP IDoc partner profile (done), 2) increase Mercury API timeout (not started). I am calling to follow up. Report your status. I will push on the incomplete item. Start the call.

Scenario 2 — 🎙️ Self-record: email dictation

Диктуй follow-up email на диктофон (2-3 мин). Включи: subject, greeting, status summary, action items, next meeting, closing.

🗣️ Диалог для отработки

Manager (You): Hi Nikolay, thanks for taking my call. I'm just following up on the two action items from Tuesday's meeting. First — the SAP IDoc partner profile fix. Was that deployed to production?

Developer: Yes, deployed on Tuesday afternoon. All 15 failed IDocs were re-processed successfully. No further issues.

Manager: Great, thanks. And the second item — the Mercury API timeout increase?

Developer: I haven't started that one yet. I've been busy with the IDoc fix and a new urgent request from the shop floor.

Manager: I see. Is there anything blocking you from starting it, or is it just a matter of prioritization?

Developer: Just prioritization. I'll work on it today.

Manager: What time can you have it done by?

Developer: By end of day, I think.

Manager: Perfect. Please confirm via email once it's done. I'll update the tracking sheet. And for the shop floor request — can you share the details? I may be able to help with prioritization.

Developer: Sure, I'll send you a quick note after this call.

Manager: Looking forward to it. Thanks, Nikolay.

Reading: Follow-Up Emails

The meeting is over, but the work is just beginning. A timely follow-up email with meeting minutes, decisions, and action items prevents confusion. Send it within 2 hours. Structure it: subject line with date and topic, key decisions, action items with owners and deadlines, next meeting date. Use bullet points for readability. A good follow-up email is a reference document people will return to.

Встреча закончена, но работа только начинается. Своевременное follow-up письмо с протоколом, решениями и поручениями предотвращает путаницу. Отправьте его в течении 2 часов. Структура: тема с датой, ключевые решения, поручения, дата следующей встречи.
