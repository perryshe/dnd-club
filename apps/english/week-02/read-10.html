Reading Day 10 В· Follow-Up Emails

Week 2 В· Р§С‚РµРЅРёРµ Рё РїРѕРЅРёРјР°РЅРёРµ С‚РµРєСЃС‚Р°

The meeting is over, but the work is just beginning. A timely follow-up email with meeting minutes, decisions, and action items prevents confusion. Send it within two hours. Structure it with a subject line containing date and topic, key decisions, action items with owners and deadlines, and the next meeting date. Use bullet points for readability. A good follow-up email is a reference document people will return to throughout the week. The subject line should include the project name and date, such as 'Meeting Notes: SAP-MES Status вЂ” 18 Jun 2026.' This makes it easy to find later in the inbox. In the body, start with a one-line summary of the meeting purpose. Then list decisions as bullet points. Then action items as a table: Action, Owner, Deadline. End with the next meeting date or a note that no meeting is scheduled. Keep the tone neutral and factual. Avoid emotional language. Copy only those who attended plus one additional stakeholder if needed.
