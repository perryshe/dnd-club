🎭 AI-практика

Scenario 1 — Angry end-user

You are a logistics specialist who is very frustrated because the Mercury system has been down for 2 hours and production can't ship. Call me (the manager) and complain. I need to acknowledge your frustration and propose a solution. You stay angry until I handle it well. Start.

Scenario 2 — Vendor complaint

You are a vendor (CRPT support) calling me. You are unhappy because our team keeps sending incorrect requests. Complain politely but firmly. I am the manager — I need to listen, acknowledge, and commit to fixing the process. Start.

🗣️ Диалог для отработки

Logistics (angry): I need to talk to someone. Mercury has been down for two hours now. We have three trucks waiting to ship and we can't generate VSDs. This is costing us money!

Manager (You): I understand your frustration. Let me look into it right now. Can you tell me exactly when it started and what error you're seeing?

Logistics: About 9 AM. The system just shows "Connection timeout" when we try to create a VSD. We've tried restarting the client — nothing.

Manager: I'm sorry to hear that. Let me check the integration log from my side. Give me two minutes.

Logistics: Okay, but please hurry. The dispatcher is calling me every 10 minutes.

Manager: I understand the urgency. I've checked the logs — the Mercury API is returning HTTP 504 errors. This looks like a service outage on their side. I'll contact Mercury support immediately and escalate. In the meantime, can you use the web interface to manually create at least one VSD?

Logistics: I tried, it's also slow but it works. I'll do what I can.

Manager: Thank you. I'll call Mercury support now and update you within 30 minutes. I appreciate your patience while we handle this.

📝 Самодиктант

  1. Я понимаю ваше разочарование.
  2. Мне жаль это слышать. Расскажите, что произошло.
  3. Я разберусь и вернусь к вам.
  4. Какое решение было бы для вас приемлемым?
  5. Спасибо за ваше терпение.

Reading: Handling Complaints

A complaint is a gift — it tells you what needs fixing. When someone complains, first listen without interrupting. Acknowledge their feeling: "I understand why you are frustrated." Apologize if appropriate. Then focus on solutions. Never get defensive. A well-handled complaint can actually strengthen the relationship more than if the problem never occurred. Follow up after resolving to close the loop.

Жалоба — это подарок: она говорит о том, что нужно исправить. Когда кто-то жалуется, сначала выслушайте, не перебивая. Признайте его чувства. Извинитесь, если уместно. Затем сосредоточьтесь на решениях. Хорошо обработанная жалоба укрепляет отношения.
