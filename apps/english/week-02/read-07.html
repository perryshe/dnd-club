Reading Day 07 В· Handling Complaints

Week 2 В· Р§С‚РµРЅРёРµ Рё РїРѕРЅРёРјР°РЅРёРµ С‚РµРєСЃС‚Р°

A complaint is a gift вЂ” it tells you what needs fixing. When someone complains, first listen without interrupting. Acknowledge their feeling: 'I understand why you are frustrated.' Apologize if appropriate. Then focus on solutions. Never get defensive. A well-handled complaint can actually strengthen the relationship more than if the problem never occurred. Follow up after resolving to close the loop. The LAA method helps: Listen, Acknowledge, Act. First, listen fully without planning your response. Second, acknowledge the person's feelings and effort in raising the issue. Third, act by defining a concrete next step. Even if you cannot solve the problem immediately, saying 'I will look into this and get back to you by Friday' shows you take it seriously. Document complaints and track patterns over time. If the same issue appears repeatedly, it is not a one-off вЂ” it is a systemic problem that needs management attention. Use complaints as data for process improvement.
