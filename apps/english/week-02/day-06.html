🎭 AI-практика

Scenario 1 — Small talk before the meeting

You are a colleague dialing into a meeting with me. Before starting the agenda, make small talk — ask about my weekend, the weather, or how things are going. I will respond briefly. After 3-4 exchanges, I will transition to business. You play along. Start.

Scenario 2 — International small talk

You are a developer from Germany and I am the manager. We haven't spoken in a week. Start with polite small talk before moving to the agenda. Keep it professional. I will match your tone. Start the conversation.

Reading: The Power of Small Talk

Small talk is the gateway to stronger workplace relationships. In international teams, it builds trust before diving into business. Safe topics include: weather, travel, weekend plans, local food, or sports. Avoid politics, religion, or personal finances. Good openers: "How was your weekend?", "Are you traveling anywhere this summer?", "Did you try the new café near the office?" Small talk shows you see colleagues as people, not just roles.

Small talk — это ворота к более крепким рабочим отношениям. В международных командах он создаёт доверие до погружения в дела. Безопасные темы: погода, путешествия, планы на выходные, местная еда или спорт. Избегайте политики, религии и личных финансов. Small talk показывает, что вы видите в коллегах людей.
